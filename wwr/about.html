    At Rapids, our purpose is to connect people with the power and beauty of the river. We offer safe, unforgettable rafting experiences led by certified guides for beginners and experts alike.

    History

    Rapids was founded in 2005 by a small group of river lovers who wanted to share their passion for white water. What started with two rafts and a borrowed van has grown into a team of over 30 guides serving thousands of adventurers every year.
